---
title: Notifications
description: Get told when a ticket is assigned to you, you are mentioned, a customer replies or an SLA is breached.
---

opendesk notifies you about the things that need you, in the app and in real time.

## Types

| type | when |
| --- | --- |
| `assigned` | A ticket is assigned to you. |
| `mentioned` | A teammate mentions you in a note. |
| `customer_replied` | A customer replies on a ticket assigned to you. |
| `sla_breached` | A ticket assigned to you misses an SLA target. |

Each notification links to its ticket. Notifications arrive live over your realtime connection, so the bell updates without refreshing.

## Reading

Open a notification to mark it read, or mark all as read at once.
