Customer satisfaction (CSAT) asks the customer to rate a conversation once it is solved. Turn it on in workspace settings.

## How it works

1. When a ticket is solved, the customer receives a short message asking how the conversation went.
2. The link opens a page where they choose a score and can add a comment. The link carries a single token, so the customer does not need an account.
3. The score and comment appear on the ticket, an activity is added to its timeline, and the score counts toward [reports](/guides/reports/).

## Using the results

- Reports show the average score and how many ratings were received.
- Low scores are a good trigger for a follow-up from a team lead.
