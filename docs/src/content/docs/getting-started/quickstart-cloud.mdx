The hosted service runs at [app.opendesk.sh](https://app.opendesk.sh). It is the same code as the self-hosted install, operated for you.

<Steps>

1. **Create an account.** Sign up with your email and a password, or with Google or Microsoft. Passwords must be 12 to 128 characters and are checked against a list of known breached passwords.

2. **Create a workspace.** A workspace holds your tickets, contacts, mailboxes and settings. You become its owner. New workspaces start with default views, tags and an SLA policy.

3. **Connect a mailbox.** Open **Settings → Mailboxes** and choose how mail comes in:
   - [Forwarding](/guides/mailboxes/forwarding/): forward your support address to the unique address opendesk gives you.
   - [Connect Google](/guides/mailboxes/google/) or [Connect Microsoft](/guides/mailboxes/microsoft/): sign in once and opendesk reads and sends through that mailbox.
   - [IMAP and SMTP](/guides/mailboxes/imap-smtp/): any other provider.

4. **Invite your team.** Open **Settings → Members** and invite people by email as an admin, agent or viewer. See [Teams and roles](/guides/teams-and-roles/).

5. **Answer your first ticket.** Send an email to your support address. It appears in the inbox within moments. Reply, and the customer receives it from your address.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Add live chat next: copy the snippet from **Settings → Chat widget** into your site. See [Live chat widget](/guides/live-chat/).
</Aside>
